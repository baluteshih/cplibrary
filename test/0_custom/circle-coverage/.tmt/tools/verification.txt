Circle coverage package verification

The statement directory is read-only. Its initial SHA-256 and nanosecond mtimes
are recorded in statement-snapshot.json and were verified unchanged.
Headers were copied from tmt-cli/examples/icpc/include as authorized.

Reference: solutions/sol.cpp, O(n^2 log n) time and O(n) working memory.
Identical circles are grouped with multiplicity. On each oriented boundary arc,
if c other disks cover it and its group has weight w, its Green integral belongs
to thresholds c+1 through c+w. A range difference array accumulates these terms.
Tangency and containment predicates use exact 64-bit integer squared distances.
The half-angle uses atan2(sqrt((sumRadius^2-d^2)*(d^2-diffRadius^2)),
d^2+r_i^2-r_j^2). Every integer intermediate fits signed 64 bits under the
statement constraints. This avoids the near-tangency cancellation of acos.
Coordinates are translated, arc sine differences use the midpoint identity,
and both difference entries and prefix sums use compensated summation.
Long double precision is platform-dependent; independent numeric checks are
included rather than assuming that it has more precision than double.

36 inputs: 2 samples, 12 geometry cases, 7 structured cases, 15 random cases.
The 500-circle cases cover identical disks, strict offset containment chains,
concentric radii, nested disks with varying multiplicities, disjoint disks,
eight weighted disks through a common point, and a heavily intersecting grid.
Random cases include small independent-oracle inputs and maximal configurations.
The same-event construction has intersections (3,+/-4) on the scanned disk
(0,0,5), shared by disks (6,0,5) and (3,0,4).

Wrong submissions (all declared wrong-answer and caught):
  deduplicate.cpp: loses multiplicities.
  exactly-k.cpp: prints exact rather than threshold coverage.
  no-wrap.cpp: drops one piece of intervals crossing the angle cut.
  no-containment.cpp: omits full circumference coverage.
  float-precision.cpp: accumulates in single precision.
  acos-near-tangent.cpp: loses small overlaps when acos rounds near 1.
The last is caught solely by geometry case 12 in the current test set.

Custom checker enforces |a-b| <= 1e-6*max(1,abs(b)) per item,
requires exactly n finite real tokens, and rejects missing and extra output.
tools/check-components.py checks 11 validator and 11 checker scenarios.

Independent oracle: tools/crosscheck.py uses Python Decimal at 65 digits,
splits vertical slabs at circle endpoints and intersection x coordinates,
and analytically integrates ordered upper/lower semicircle strips. Its atan
is evaluated by argument reduction and a convergent Taylor series. Both
internal and external tangencies are slab cuts. There is no dependency on
mpmath, numerical quadrature, Monte Carlo, or the library under test.
It checks 119 cases, 580 oracle/metamorphic comparisons, analytic maximal
nested/disjoint cases, monotonicity and the multiplicity-weighted area sum.
Oracle/metamorphic tolerance is 1e-7*max(1,abs(expected)); the library uses
the statement tolerance 1e-6. See crosscheck-report.json for measured results.
